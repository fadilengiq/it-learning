🔐 Cybersecurity

Learn the fundamentals of cybersecurity and information protection

What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications, systems, and data from unauthorized access, attacks, damage, or disruption.

As organizations increasingly depend on digital technologies, cybersecurity has become an important part of modern information technology.

The CIA Triad

The CIA triad is a fundamental security model consisting of three important principles.

🔒 Confidentiality

Confidentiality ensures that information is accessible only to authorized users and systems.

✅ Integrity

Integrity ensures that information remains accurate and is not modified without authorization.

🌐 Availability

Availability ensures that authorized users can access systems and information when they are needed.

Common Cybersecurity Threats

🎣 Phishing

Phishing attempts use deceptive messages or websites to trick users into revealing sensitive information.

🦠 Malware

Malware is malicious software designed to damage systems, disrupt operations, or gain unauthorized access.

🔑 Password Attacks

Attackers may attempt to obtain or guess passwords in order to access protected accounts.

💻 Social Engineering

Social engineering uses manipulation or deception to influence people into performing actions that may compromise security.

Authentication

Authentication is the process of verifying the identity of a user, device, or system before allowing access.

🔑 Passwords

Passwords are one of the most common methods used to verify a user's identity.

📱 Multi-Factor Authentication

Multi-factor authentication requires more than one type of verification, providing an additional layer of security.

Basic Security Practices

  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication when available.
  • Keep operating systems and applications updated.
  • Be careful when opening unexpected links or attachments.
  • Back up important data regularly.
  • Avoid sharing sensitive information unnecessarily.

Network Security

Network security focuses on protecting communication networks and the systems connected to them.

🧱 Firewall

A firewall can monitor and control network traffic according to defined security rules.

🔐 Encryption

Encryption transforms information into a protected form so that it cannot be easily understood without the appropriate key or mechanism.

Learning Objectives

After studying this lesson, you should be able to:

  • Explain the concept of cybersecurity.
  • Describe the CIA triad.
  • Identify common cybersecurity threats.
  • Explain the purpose of authentication.
  • Describe basic cybersecurity practices.
  • Understand the role of firewalls and encryption.

Quick Question

Which security principle ensures that information is accessible only to authorized users?

Answer: Confidentiality
